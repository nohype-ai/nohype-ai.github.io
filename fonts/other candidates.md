## Grok Chat

Keep SF Pro for nav, body, and UI. It is the Apple signal, and it already works at 16px.

Use one accent, not all three.

- **Instrument Serif** is the headline only. “AI / needs / Nohype” at 80px, maybe the yellow H2 links. That is the contrast.
  - https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif
- **Satoshi** does not earn a role next to SF Pro. Same job, softer geometry. Use it only if you drop SF Pro entirely.
  - https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/satoshi

Ship SF Pro plus Instrument Serif.

## Brave Ask Addition

Space Grotesk for headings + SF Pro for body. It's the strongest "modern cool" contrast — enough personality to feel intentional, but not so loud it fights the clean tech aesthetic. The slightly irregular letterforms (especially the a, g, e) give it that "designed by someone who cares" vibe that pure SF Pro lacks.
  - https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk

## Grok Build Additions

Keep SF Pro on nav, paragraphs, names, and footer. Put one other face on the `h1` posters and the `h2` titles.

- **Instrument Serif** if the poster should be narrower and sharper. Drawn for large sizes. Only weight 400, so `h1` has to drop the browser bold. Already a very common choice.
  - https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif
- **Satoshi** on the regular text and on `h1` and `h2`. Plain grotesque, weight 300–900. Body at 400, titles at 700.
  - https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/satoshi

Ship Satoshi for the text and the titles.
