# Bottom navigation

A sticky bottom bar is not worth doing.

The top of this site is already an entry: the poster and the email. A bar that stays on screen at the bottom becomes a second entry, equal in presence. The page then has two fronts, and the reading direction stops being top to bottom. Legal and Imprint sit in that same bar, so they take on the weight of the content links. The scroll used to be the quiet place. A sticky bar deletes it.

## Comparison

| Aspect | What a sticky bottom bar does | What this site needs | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Attention | The top and the bottom are both present for the whole visit. Both get designed, so both read as a start. | One front. The poster is the start, the email is the step. | Bottom bar loses. Two starts split the page. |
| Reading direction | The end of the page is pinned in view before the page has been read. The eye finishes at the top and is offered another beginning. | A direction. Offer, then proof, then the quiet end. | Bottom bar loses. |
| Primary and secondary | One persistent row gives every item the same presence. Left and right is a weak demotion. The shared bar is the strong signal, and it says each item is navigation. | Content links carry the second click. Legal and Imprint must be findable and must look quieter than that click. | Bottom bar loses. Secondary things need a place that can leave the screen. |
| The visit | A bottom bar pays for itself when people switch among a few sections over and over. That is an app session. | A visitor reads, maybe opens Team or Projects, and writes. Most sessions are one or two pages. | Bottom bar loses. The instrument is built for a session this site does not have. |
| What the chrome says about the firm | A sticky bottom row is the language of a product you operate. On a wide screen it also sits where banners and chat widgets sit. | A firm someone hires. The unusual part is the sentence, the terms, and the absence of junk. The menu can be the ordinary instrument. | Bottom bar loses. It categorizes the firm before the sentence does. |
| Legal reachability | A fixed Imprint link is always one click away. | Imprint has to be recognizable and easy to reach. It does not have to share the spotlight with the content links. | A link at the end of the page, present from every page, is enough. |
| The top of the page | The poster gets the whole top. | The poster matters. It does not need the bottom to become a second poster. | A quiet line of content links at the top spends less than a second front. |

## Verdict

Not worth doing. The bar would stick, and that is the problem. Presence is importance. A row that never leaves promotes everything in it, flattens Legal into the same class as the content links, and gives the page a second front under a poster that is already the front.

Content links go in one quiet line at the top, on every screen. Legal, then Imprint, go at the end of the page and scroll with it. The copyright and later policies live on the Legal page. New policies do not add links.

## What that means in the page

- One reading direction. The content links, the logo, the page, then Legal and Imprint.
- The content line is not a bar with a background, a second logo, or a call to action. It is the links.
- The legal links are not fixed. They leave with the page, which is what keeps them secondary.
- Imprint stays its own word, one click from every page.
- A new policy goes on the Legal page.
